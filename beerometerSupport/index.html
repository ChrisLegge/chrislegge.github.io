Beerometer — Support

Questions, troubleshooting, and how to get in touch

Getting Started

Log a beer by tapping the button in the app, the Home Screen widget, or the button on your Apple Watch — no account or sign-up needed, it works with your existing iCloud account. Your data syncs automatically between your iPhone and Apple Watch.

Frequently Asked Questions

I logged a beer by mistake — how do I undo it?
Tap "Undo" on the Log screen, the widget, or the Watch app to remove the most recently logged entry.

Why isn't the widget updating instantly?
Widgets refresh periodically rather than instantly — open the app to always see the latest count immediately.

How does the leaderboard work?
Enter a display name and make up a group code, then share that code with friends — anyone who enters the same code shows up on the same leaderboard, ranked by total beers. Leave a group at any time from the Leaderboard tab to stop appearing on it.

Is my data private?
Yes, by default. Your log entries and location are stored privately in your own iCloud account and are never seen by anyone else. Only what you choose to share by joining a leaderboard group becomes visible to others with that code. See the Privacy Policy for full details.

The map is empty — why?
Location is optional. Make sure Beerometer has location permission enabled in your device's Settings, and that you've logged at least one beer since granting it.

The Live Activity / Dynamic Island isn't showing up.
Make sure Live Activities are enabled for Beerometer in Settings → Beerometer, and that Live Activities are enabled system-wide under Settings → Face ID & Passcode (or Settings → Notifications → Live Activities, depending on iOS version).
