Custom Lightsaber

February 2025 – March 2025

Overview

I built a fully functional lightsaber using an Arduino microcontroller and WS2812B LEDs that can be individually addressed to create animations through my code. I added switches and buttons for controls and used professional workshop equipment to create the handle.

The Handle

The handle is mainly made from two tubes of PVC pipe, one fitting completely inside the other. After making a design, I cut the PVC with a hacksaw and used a belt sander to get the pieces smooth. For the parts with details or bevels I used the lathe to cut into the PVC evenly and get the details right. For the grip strips I used a laser cutter and a sheet of 3mm acrylic to get an accurate shape. I then used a polymer welding glue to put all the pieces together after spray painting them the right colour, with added details such as dirt for wear and tear. I also designed a small circle to cover the bottom so there would be no hole at the end of the PVC pipe.

The Blade

The blade was made from a spare blade that I bought cheap from a lightsaber shop. I added two strips of WS2812B LEDs, soldered end to end so they were part of the same strip, and stuck them back to back with a wooden pole through the middle so the blade didn't collapse. I covered the LEDs in packing foam as a cheap diffuser and wired the three pins to a removable connector, with the other end soldered to an Arduino alongside a battery, a switch and a button. After some programming and a lot of testing I could fit all the electronics inside the lightsaber. A switch on the back cuts power to the battery, with a removable panel to change it; an on/off switch programmed into the Arduino triggers an animation that slides up or down the blade; and a button changes the colour. I am going to add other animations, which I have already programmed, for when the button is pressed and held.
