Predictive Bus Routing

November 2025 – March 2026

Overview

As a team we designed a system to increase the efficiency of buses using a machine learning algorithm. It uses accurate data modelled from across the UK to predict the footfall at each bus stop and dynamically reroute buses to pick up passengers, so that you spend less time waiting and less time travelling.

Physical Board

My main part of the project was to make the physical map, so that people at bus stops could see when the next bus will arrive. I originally designed a custom PCB for this part of the project; however, this was out of budget for our small project, so instead I soldered the light map together from a long strip of LEDs. I then added the machine learning model to my Arduino so that you could see the buses moving on the map, and repeated this using Verilog and an FPGA board so that we could run the different settings and it could run faster.

Results & Reflections

The project was built for the Engineers Without Borders UK Design Challenge. From hundreds of entries across the UK, our team was selected as one of just 36 Grand Finalists invited to present at the national grand finals in June 2026. The project required careful planning from every member of the group to get it done on time: we created a Gantt chart to stay on track and assigned tasks to everyone's strengths, which is why our project management was so successful. Overall we worked well together as a team, and the project management played to each of our strengths.
